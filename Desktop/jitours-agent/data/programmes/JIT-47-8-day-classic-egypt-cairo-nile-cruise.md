The pyramids, the Grand Egyptian Museum and four nights sailing the Nile from Aswan to Luxor, with both internal flights included.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Cairo

Upon your arrival at Cairo International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Egypt visa and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Cairo for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Pyramids of Giza – Sphinx – Grand Egyptian Museum

The Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx with an Egyptologist guide, then the Grand Egyptian Museum and the treasures of Tutankhamun.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Pyramids of Giza · The Sphinx · Grand Egyptian Museum

### Day 3 – Fly Cairo – Aswan, board the Nile cruise

Morning flight to Aswan. Visit the High Dam and the Philae Temple by boat, then board your cruise.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Philae Temple · High Dam · Felucca at sunset

### Day 4 – Nile cruise: Kom Ombo – Edfu

Sail north to the double temple of Kom Ombo and the Temple of Horus at Edfu.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Kom Ombo Temple · Edfu Temple · Sailing the Nile

### Day 5 – Luxor East Bank

Arrive in Luxor and visit the Karnak and Luxor temples.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Karnak Temple · Luxor Temple · Avenue of Sphinxes

### Day 6 – Luxor West Bank

The Valley of the Kings, the Temple of Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Valley of the Kings · Temple of Hatshepsut · Colossi of Memnon

### Day 7 – Fly Luxor – Cairo, Khan El-Khalili

Disembark and fly to Cairo. Afternoon in Islamic Cairo and the Khan El-Khalili bazaar.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Khan El-Khalili · Islamic Cairo · Al-Muizz Street

### Day 8 – Cairo – departure

Transfer to Cairo International Airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
