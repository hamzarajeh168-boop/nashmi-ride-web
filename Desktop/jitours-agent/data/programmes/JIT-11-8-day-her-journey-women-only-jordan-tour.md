Travel with a female guide, meet Jordanian women entrepreneurs, cook with a local family and relax in a women-friendly spa at the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Jerash – Amman cooking class

Visit Jerash with your female guide, then return to Amman for a hands-on cooking class with a local family, preparing maqluba and mezze, followed by dinner together.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Jerash, Hadrian's Gate · Cooking class with a local family · Dinner with the family

### Day 3 – Amman – Madaba women's cooperative – Petra

Visit a women-run mosaic workshop in Madaba and the St. George Church, then Mount Nebo. Continue to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Women's mosaic cooperative · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo

### Day 4 – Petra guided tour & overnight

Explore Petra with your female guide, then visit a Bedouin women's handicraft project in Umm Sayhoun. Dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Bedouin handicrafts · Petra by night (optional)

### Day 5 – Petra – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari with a woman-friendly Bedouin host family, dinner and a night in private tents.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Bedouin tea · Stargazing

### Day 6 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba

Morning in the desert, then drive to Aqaba for a snorkelling boat trip and a free evening.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Glass-bottom boat · Snorkelling · Aqaba souk

### Day 7 – Aqaba – Dead Sea spa

Drive to the Dead Sea for a spa afternoon with a mud wrap included, floating and a farewell dinner.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mud wrap at the spa · Floating, Dead Sea · Farewell dinner

### Day 8 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
