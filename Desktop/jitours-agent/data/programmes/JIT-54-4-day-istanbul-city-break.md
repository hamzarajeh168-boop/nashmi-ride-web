Two continents in one city: the great mosques of Sultanahmet, a Bosphorus cruise, the bazaars and a day on the car-free Princes' Islands.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Istanbul

Upon your arrival at Istanbul Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Istanbul for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Sultanahmet

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern and Topkapı Palace with a licensed guide.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Hagia Sophia · Blue Mosque · Basilica Cistern

### Day 3 – Bosphorus – Princes' Islands

A morning ferry to Büyükada on the Princes' Islands, then an evening Bosphorus cruise.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Princes' Islands · Bosphorus cruise · Dinner with a view

### Day 4 – Grand Bazaar – departure

Morning in the Grand Bazaar, then transfer to Istanbul Airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Grand Bazaar · Spice Bazaar · Departure
