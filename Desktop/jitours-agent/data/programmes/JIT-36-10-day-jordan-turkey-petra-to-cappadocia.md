Jordan's classic loop followed by Istanbul's mosques and bazaars and the fairy chimneys of Cappadocia, with both internal flights included.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Petra

Amman Citadel and the Madaba mosaics, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · King's Highway

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum

Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then a 2-hour jeep safari and a night in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea

Drive to the Dead Sea for floating and a relaxing afternoon.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath · Resort pools

### Day 5 – Fly Amman – Istanbul

Transfer to Amman airport for the flight to Istanbul (about 2.5 hrs.). Evening free in Sultanahmet.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Istanbul · Sultanahmet · Evening free

### Day 6 – Istanbul old city

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern and Topkapı Palace with a licensed guide.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Hagia Sophia · Blue Mosque · Topkapı Palace

### Day 7 – Bosphorus cruise – Grand Bazaar

A Bosphorus boat cruise, the Spice Bazaar and the Grand Bazaar.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Bosphorus cruise · Spice Bazaar · Grand Bazaar

### Day 8 – Fly Istanbul – Cappadocia

Morning flight to Kayseri and transfer to your cave hotel. Afternoon in the Göreme Open-Air Museum.

**Overnight:** Cappadocia (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Cappadocia · Göreme Open-Air Museum · Cave hotel

### Day 9 – Cappadocia valleys & underground city

Walk the Rose and Red valleys, visit an underground city and a pottery workshop in Avanos.

**Overnight:** Cappadocia (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Rose Valley · Underground city · Avanos pottery

### Day 10 – Cappadocia – Istanbul airport

Flight back to Istanbul to connect with your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
