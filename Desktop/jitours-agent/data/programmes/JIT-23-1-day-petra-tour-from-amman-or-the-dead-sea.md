## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Petra guided tour – Amman

Pick-up at 6:30 AM and drive the Desert Highway to Petra (3 hrs.). Explore with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours through the Siq, the Treasury, the Street of Facades and the Theatre, followed by free time and lunch inside Petra. Drive back to arrive at about 7:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** The Siq, Petra · The Treasury, Petra · Lunch inside Petra
