Timed for the best light: sunrise at the Treasury, golden hour at the Monastery, the Milky Way over Wadi Rum and salt formations at the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman street photography – Petra

Morning street photography in downtown Amman, then drive to Petra for a sunset view over Wadi Musa.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Downtown Amman · Street portraits · Sunset, Wadi Musa

### Day 3 – Petra at sunrise & golden hour

Enter Petra at opening time to photograph an empty Siq and the Treasury in early light, then climb to the Monastery for golden hour.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Empty Siq at sunrise · The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery at golden hour

### Day 4 – Little Petra – Wadi Rum, night sky

Little Petra in the morning, then Wadi Rum for sunset on the dunes and a guided night-sky session for Milky Way photography.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Little Petra · Dune sunset · Milky Way session

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum sunrise – Dead Sea

Sunrise over the desert, then drive to the Dead Sea to photograph salt formations at sunset.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Desert sunrise · Salt formations · Dead Sea sunset

### Day 6 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast and transfer to Queen Alia airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
