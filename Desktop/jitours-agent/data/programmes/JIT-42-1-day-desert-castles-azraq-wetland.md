## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Desert Castles – Azraq – Amman

Pick-up at 8:00 AM. Visit Qasr Al-Kharana, the frescoed bathhouse of Qusayr Amra (UNESCO) and Qasr Al-Azraq, where Lawrence of Arabia stayed. Walk the boardwalk of the Azraq Wetland Reserve and return at about 4:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** Qusayr Amra frescoes · Qasr Al-Azraq · Azraq Wetland
