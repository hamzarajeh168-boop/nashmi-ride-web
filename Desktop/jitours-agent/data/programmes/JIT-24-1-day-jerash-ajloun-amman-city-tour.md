## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Jerash – Ajloun – Amman city tour

Pick-up at 8:00 AM. Visit Jerash with a site guide for 1.5 hours, then Ajloun Castle. Lunch at a local restaurant, then a panoramic tour of Amman: the Citadel, the Roman Theatre and Rainbow Street. Return at about 5:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch
