Three nights in Aqaba with four boat dives on wrecks and coral gardens, plus Petra and Wadi Rum.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Aqaba

Meet and assist at Aqaba King Hussein International Airport (direct flights from Europe in winter) and transfer to your hotel. Evening walk on the corniche.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** Arrival in Aqaba · Aqaba corniche · Red Sea sunset

### Day 2 – Two boat dives: Cedar Pride & Japanese Garden

A full day with a PADI dive centre: the Cedar Pride wreck and the Japanese Garden reef. Non-divers can snorkel from the boat.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Cedar Pride wreck · Japanese Garden · Coral and turtles

### Day 3 – Two dives: Tank & Military Museum underwater

Morning dives at the underwater military museum (a sunken tank and aircraft) and the Rainbow Reef. Free afternoon.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Underwater military museum · Rainbow Reef · Free afternoon

### Day 4 – Aqaba – Wadi Rum jeep tour – Petra

Wait 24 hours after diving before high altitude: a morning 2-hour jeep tour in Wadi Rum (low altitude), then on to Petra in the afternoon.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Lawrence Spring · Wadi Musa

### Day 5 – Petra guided tour – Aqaba airport

Explore Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours, then drive back to Aqaba airport (2 hrs.) for your evening flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Royal Tombs · Departure
