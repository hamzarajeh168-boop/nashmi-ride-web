Five days in Jordan, then Cairo's pyramids and a 3-night Nile cruise from Aswan to Luxor, with the internal flight Amman–Cairo included.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

Amman Citadel, Madaba mosaics and Mount Nebo, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum

Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep tour and a night in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea

Drive to the Dead Sea for floating and a relaxing afternoon.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath · Resort pools

### Day 5 – Fly Amman – Cairo

Transfer to Amman airport for the 1.5-hour flight to Cairo. Meet and assist with the Egyptian visa, transfer to your hotel near the pyramids.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Cairo · Egypt visa assistance · Pyramids view

### Day 6 – Pyramids of Giza – Sphinx – Grand Egyptian Museum

Visit the Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx with an Egyptologist guide, then the Grand Egyptian Museum with the treasures of Tutankhamun.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Pyramids of Giza · The Sphinx · Grand Egyptian Museum

### Day 7 – Fly Cairo – Aswan, Nile cruise

Early flight to Aswan and board your Nile cruise. Visit the High Dam and the Philae Temple by boat.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Philae Temple · High Dam · Felucca at sunset

### Day 8 – Nile cruise: Kom Ombo – Edfu

Sail north and visit the double temple of Kom Ombo and the Temple of Horus at Edfu.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Kom Ombo Temple · Edfu Temple · Sailing the Nile

### Day 9 – Luxor: Valley of the Kings – Karnak

Visit the Valley of the Kings, the Temple of Hatshepsut and the Colossi of Memnon on the West Bank, then Karnak and Luxor temples.

**Overnight:** Nile cruise (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Valley of the Kings · Karnak Temple · Luxor Temple

### Day 10 – Luxor – Cairo

Disembark and fly back to Cairo. Afternoon in the Khan El-Khalili bazaar and Islamic Cairo.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Khan El-Khalili · Islamic Cairo · Farewell dinner

### Day 11 – Cairo – departure

Breakfast and transfer to Cairo International Airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
