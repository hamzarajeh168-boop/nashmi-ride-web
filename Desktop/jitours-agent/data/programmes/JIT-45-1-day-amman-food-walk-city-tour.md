## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman Citadel – downtown food walk – Rainbow Street

Pick-up at 10:00 AM. Visit the Citadel and the Roman Theatre with your guide, then a food walk through downtown with eight tastings: falafel, hummus, ka'ak, knafeh, fresh juice and Arabic coffee. Finish on Rainbow Street at about 4:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Roman Theatre · Downtown food walk
