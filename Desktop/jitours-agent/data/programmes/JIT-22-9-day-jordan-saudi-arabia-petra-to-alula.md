From Petra, the Nabataean capital, overland to Hegra, its southern sister city in AlUla, Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO site.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

Amman Citadel, Madaba and Mount Nebo, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour & overnight

A full day in Petra with a site guide, focusing on the Nabataean tombs to compare with Hegra later.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Royal Tombs · The Monastery, Petra

### Day 4 – Petra – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari and a night in the desert.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Nabataean inscriptions · Desert camp

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba

Drive to Aqaba for a boat trip on the Red Sea and a free afternoon.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Glass-bottom boat · Red Sea · Aqaba Castle

### Day 6 – Durra border – Haql – Tabuk

Cross into Saudi Arabia at the Durra/Haql border with assistance. Drive along the Gulf of Aqaba coast to Tabuk; visit Tabuk Castle and the Hijaz Railway station.

**Overnight:** Tabuk (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Haql coast · Tabuk Castle · Hijaz Railway station

### Day 7 – Tabuk – AlUla, Elephant Rock

Drive south to AlUla (4 hrs.). Sunset at Elephant Rock (Jabal Al-Fil).

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Desert drive · AlUla Old Town · Elephant Rock at sunset

### Day 8 – Hegra – Dadan – Jabal Ikmah

Visit Hegra (Mada'in Salih) with its 111 Nabataean tombs, then the ancient kingdom of Dadan and the inscriptions library of Jabal Ikmah.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Hegra tombs · Dadan · Jabal Ikmah

### Day 9 – AlUla – departure

Breakfast and transfer to AlUla International Airport for your flight (connections via Riyadh or Jeddah).

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
