The two icons of Jordan in 48 hours: a guided day in Petra and a night under the stars in Wadi Rum.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum, camping

Early pick-up from your Amman hotel and a 3-hour drive south along the Desert Highway to Petra. Explore the Rose City with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours: the Siq, the Treasury, the Street of Facades and the Royal Tombs, then enjoy free time. In the late afternoon drive to Wadi Rum for a traditional Bedouin dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** The Siq, Petra · The Treasury, Petra · Bedouin dinner, Wadi Rum

### Day 2 – Wadi Rum jeep safari – Amman

After breakfast at the camp, enjoy a 2-hour 4x4 jeep safari among red dunes, the Lawrence Spring, Khazali Canyon and natural rock bridges. Then drive back north to Amman, arriving in the late afternoon at your hotel or the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** 4x4 safari, Wadi Rum · Khazali Canyon · Red sand dunes
