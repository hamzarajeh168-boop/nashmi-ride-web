Oman's best in six days: Muscat's Grand Mosque, swimming in Wadi Shab, a night in the Wahiba dunes, Nizwa Fort and the terraces of Jebel Akhdar.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Muscat

Upon your arrival at Muscat International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Muscat for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Muscat city tour

The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, the Royal Opera House, the Muttrah souk and the corniche.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque · Muttrah souk · Royal Opera House

### Day 3 – Bimmah Sinkhole – Wadi Shab – Wahiba Sands

The Bimmah Sinkhole, a swim in the turquoise pools of Wadi Shab, then into the Wahiba Sands for a dune drive and camp night.

**Overnight:** Wahiba Sands (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Bimmah Sinkhole · Wadi Shab pools · Dune drive

### Day 4 – Wahiba Sands – Nizwa

Sunrise on the dunes, then Nizwa and its 17th-century fort.

**Overnight:** Nizwa (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Desert sunrise · Nizwa Fort · Nizwa souk

### Day 5 – Jebel Akhdar – Misfat Al Abriyeen

A day in the Green Mountain: rose terraces, the village of Misfat Al Abriyeen and the falaj irrigation channels.

**Overnight:** Nizwa (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Jebel Akhdar terraces · Misfat Al Abriyeen · Falaj irrigation

### Day 6 – Nizwa – Muscat airport

Drive back to Muscat (1.5 hrs.) for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
