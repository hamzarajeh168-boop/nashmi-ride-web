The classic route planned for reduced mobility: an adapted vehicle with a ramp, accessible rooms and a Petra visit by electric golf cart.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Meet and assist at Queen Alia airport with wheelchair assistance, then transfer in the adapted vehicle to your accessible room in Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Wheelchair assistance · Adapted vehicle

### Day 2 – Amman Citadel – Madaba – Petra

Visit the Amman Citadel (paved paths), the mosaic map in Madaba (step-free entry) and drive to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · King's Highway

### Day 3 – Petra by electric cart – Wadi Rum

Ride an electric golf cart through the Siq to the Treasury and the colonnaded street with your guide. Afternoon drive to an accessible camp in Wadi Rum.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Electric cart through the Siq · The Treasury, Petra · Accessible desert camp

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea

A gentle jeep drive at sunrise, then on to the Dead Sea, where the resort has a beach wheelchair and a pool hoist.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Sunrise jeep drive · Beach wheelchair · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 5 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast and transfer to the airport with wheelchair assistance.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
