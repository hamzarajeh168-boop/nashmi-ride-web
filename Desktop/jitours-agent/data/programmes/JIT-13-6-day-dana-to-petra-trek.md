The famous trail from the Dana Biosphere Reserve to the back door of Petra, with nights in an ecolodge and Bedouin camps, ending at the Monastery.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Dana village, White Dome trail

Drive south to Dana village (3 hrs.). Afternoon acclimatisation walk on the White Dome trail with views across Wadi Dana. Overnight in Dana.

**Overnight:** Dana (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Dana village · White Dome trail · Wadi Dana views

### Day 3 – Trek: Dana – Feynan Ecolodge (14 km)

Descend 1,200 m through the Dana valley past sandstone and granite to the candle-lit Feynan Ecolodge. Lunch box on the trail.

**Overnight:** Feynan (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Dana Valley descent · Feynan Ecolodge · Rooftop stargazing

### Day 4 – Trek: Feynan – Ras Al-Feid (16 km)

A long walking day through Wadi Ghuweir's narrow canyon and Bedouin grazing lands. Camp with a local Bedouin family.

**Overnight:** Bedouin wild camp (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Ghuweir canyon · Bedouin shepherds · Wild camp

### Day 5 – Trek: Ras Al-Feid – Little Petra (18 km)

Cross the mountain plateau to Little Petra, then transfer to your hotel in Wadi Musa for a well-earned shower.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Mountain plateau · Little Petra · Hot shower!

### Day 6 – Trek: Little Petra – the Monastery – Petra – Amman

Enter Petra by the back trail to arrive at the Monastery, walk down through the city to the Treasury and out through the Siq. Drive back to Amman in the evening.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Back trail to the Monastery · The Treasury, Petra · The Siq
