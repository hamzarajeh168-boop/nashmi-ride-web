Adds Roman Jerash and Ajloun Castle to the classic route, with two nights in Petra for an unhurried visit.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Ajloun – Jerash, guided tour – Amman

After breakfast, drive north to Ajloun Castle, a 12th-century Muslim fortress, then to Jerash, one of the best-preserved Roman cities outside Italy. An English-speaking site guide shows you the main sites for 1.5 hours, followed by free time. Return to Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Jerash, Hadrian's Gate · The Oval Plaza, Jerash · Ajloun Castle

### Day 3 – Amman Citadel – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

Visit the Amman Citadel, then Madaba and its mosaic map of the Holy Land, and Mount Nebo. Continue south to Petra for dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo, Moses Memorial

### Day 4 – Petra guided tour & overnight

Explore Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours: the Treasury, the Street of Facades, the Theatre, the Nymphaeum and Qasr Al-Bint. Spend the afternoon exploring at your own pace, then return to the hotel for dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery, Petra · Petra by night (optional)

### Day 5 – Little Petra – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Visit Little Petra and its Nabataean caves, then drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari, dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Little Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 6 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast at the camp, then drive to the Dead Sea for floating and a mud bath at a resort day-use, before your transfer to Amman airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Dead Sea shores · Departure
