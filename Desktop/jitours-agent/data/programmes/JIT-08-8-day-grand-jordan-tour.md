North to south: Roman cities, the Baptism Site, crusader castles, two days in Petra, the desert and the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Jerash – Umm Qais – Amman

Visit Roman Jerash with an English-speaking site guide for 1.5 hours, then continue to Umm Qais (Gadara) overlooking the Sea of Galilee and the Golan Heights. Return to Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Jerash, Hadrian's Gate · Umm Qais, Gadara · View over the Sea of Galilee

### Day 3 – Amman city tour – Ajloun – Amman

Morning in Amman: the Citadel, the Roman Theatre and the downtown souks. In the afternoon drive to Ajloun Castle and the forest reserve, then return to Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Roman Theatre, Amman · Ajloun Castle

### Day 4 – Bethany – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Kerak – Petra

Visit Bethany Beyond the Jordan, the Baptism Site of Jesus (UNESCO), then Madaba, Mount Nebo and Kerak Castle on the King's Highway. Dinner and overnight in Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Baptism Site, Bethany · Mosaic, Madaba · Kerak Castle

### Day 5 – Petra guided tour & overnight

Explore Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours, followed by free time. Dinner and overnight in Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Street of Facades · Royal Tombs

### Day 6 – Petra Monastery – Shobak – Little Petra – Wadi Rum

Early morning hike to the Monastery via the back trail from Little Petra, then visit Shobak Castle. Drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari, dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Monastery, Petra · Shobak Castle · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum

### Day 7 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea

After breakfast, drive to the Dead Sea for relaxation, floating and swimming. Dinner and overnight at the resort.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Dead Sea shores · Mud bath, Dead Sea

### Day 8 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
