## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman: Cave of the Seven Sleepers – King Abdullah I Mosque – Citadel

Visit Ahl Al-Kahf (the Cave of the Seven Sleepers mentioned in Surat Al-Kahf), the blue-domed King Abdullah I Mosque and the Umayyad Palace at the Amman Citadel.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Cave of the Seven Sleepers · King Abdullah I Mosque · Umayyad Palace

### Day 3 – Jordan Valley shrines of the Companions – Ajloun

Visit the shrines of Abu Ubaidah Amer Ibn Al-Jarrah, Mu'adh Ibn Jabal, Shurahbil Ibn Hasana and Dirar Ibn Al-Azwar in the Jordan Valley, then Ajloun Castle built by Saladin's commander.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Shrine of Abu Ubaidah · Shrine of Mu'adh Ibn Jabal · Ajloun Castle

### Day 4 – Mutah – Kerak – Petra

Visit Mutah and the shrines of the martyrs of the Battle of Mutah: Zaid Ibn Haritha, Ja'far Ibn Abi Talib and Abdullah Ibn Rawaha. Continue via Kerak Castle to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Shrine of Ja'far Al-Tayyar · Mutah battlefield · Kerak Castle

### Day 5 – Petra – Prophet Aaron's mountain view – Dead Sea

Explore Petra with a guide for 2.5 hours, viewing Jebel Haroun (Prophet Aaron). Drive to the Dead Sea, the site of the people of Prophet Lut.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Jebel Haroun · Dead Sea

### Day 6 – Dead Sea – Prophet Shuayb shrine – Amman airport

Visit the shrine of Prophet Shuayb in Wadi Shuayb, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Shrine of Prophet Shuayb · Jordan Valley · Departure
