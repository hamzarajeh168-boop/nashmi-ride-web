Jordan's biblical sites and Petra plus two nights in Jerusalem with Bethlehem and Jericho, crossing the King Hussein (Allenby) Bridge.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Jerash – Ajloun – Amman city tour

Visit Jerash with a site guide for 1.5 hours and Ajloun Castle, then an afternoon tour of the Amman Citadel and the Roman Theatre.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Jerash, Hadrian's Gate · Ajloun Castle · Roman Theatre, Amman

### Day 3 – Allenby Bridge – Jericho – Mount of Olives – Jerusalem

Cross the King Hussein (Allenby) Bridge with assistance. Visit Jericho and the Mount of Temptation, then the Mount of Olives and the Garden of Gethsemane. Overnight in Jerusalem.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Jericho · Mount of Olives · Garden of Gethsemane

### Day 4 – Jerusalem Old City

A full day in the Old City: the Via Dolorosa, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, the Western Wall, the Cardo and Mount Zion with the Room of the Last Supper.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Via Dolorosa · Church of the Holy Sepulchre · Western Wall

### Day 5 – Bethlehem – Allenby Bridge – Dead Sea

Visit the Church of the Nativity and Shepherds' Field in Bethlehem, then cross back to Jordan and drive to the Dead Sea.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Church of the Nativity · Shepherds' Field · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 6 – Bethany – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

The Baptism Site at Bethany, the Madaba mosaic map and Mount Nebo, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Baptism Site, Bethany · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo

### Day 7 – Petra guided tour – Amman

Explore Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, lunch inside Petra, free time, then drive back to Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Lunch inside Petra · The Monastery (optional)

### Day 8 – Amman – airport, departure

Breakfast and transfer to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
