The most dramatic stretch of the Jordan Trail: from the Monastery of Petra across empty valleys to the red dunes of Wadi Rum, sleeping in wild camps.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Petra guided tour

Drive to Petra and explore with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then prepare for the trek with your mountain guide.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Trek briefing · Wadi Musa

### Day 3 – Trek: Petra Monastery – Wadi Feid (15 km)

Climb to the Monastery and leave Petra by the back trail, descending into the wild Wadi Feid. First night in a wild camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** The Monastery, Petra · Back trail · Wild camp

### Day 4 – Trek: Wadi Feid – Gaa Mreirat (18 km)

A long day across sandstone plateaus and Bedouin grazing land.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Sandstone plateau · Bedouin shepherds · Stargazing

### Day 5 – Trek: Gaa Mreirat – Wadi Rum edge (16 km)

Walk into the granite and sand landscape that marks the edge of Wadi Rum.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Granite peaks · Red dunes · Wild camp

### Day 6 – Trek: Wadi Rum – Rum village (14 km)

Final trekking day through the canyons of Wadi Rum to a Bedouin camp with showers.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Khazali Canyon · Rum village · Bedouin camp

### Day 7 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba or Amman airport

Transfer to Aqaba or back to Amman airport for your flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the trek
