Follow the footsteps of Moses, Elijah, John the Baptist and Jesus through the Holy Land on the east bank of the Jordan River.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Jerash – Anjara – Amman

Visit Jerash (Gerasa), one of the cities of the Decapolis, then the Shrine of Our Lady of the Mountain in Anjara, where Jesus and Mary are believed to have rested. Return to Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Jerash, Decapolis city · Our Lady of the Mountain, Anjara · Amman Citadel

### Day 3 – Bethany Beyond the Jordan – Mt. Nebo – Madaba

Visit the Baptism Site of Jesus with time to renew baptismal vows at the river, then Mount Nebo and Madaba's mosaic map of the Holy Land.

**Overnight:** Madaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Baptism Site, Bethany · Mt. Nebo, Moses Memorial · Mosaic map, Madaba

### Day 4 – Mukawir (Machaerus) – Kerak – Petra

Visit Mukawir, the hilltop fortress of Herod where John the Baptist was beheaded, then Kerak Castle and on to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Machaerus fortress · Kerak Castle · King's Highway

### Day 5 – Petra – Aaron's Tomb view – Dead Sea

Explore Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, with a view of Jebel Haroun, the traditional tomb of Aaron. Drive to the Dead Sea.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Jebel Haroun view · Dead Sea

### Day 6 – Lot's Cave – Amman airport, departure

Visit the Monastery of Lot near the Dead Sea and the Museum at the Lowest Place on Earth, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Lot's Cave · Lowest Place on Earth museum · Departure
