## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Jerash Roman chariot show – Amman

Visit Jerash and watch the Roman Army and Chariot Experience in the Hippodrome (legionaries, gladiators and chariot races). Free afternoon at the hotel pool.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Roman chariot show, Jerash · Hippodrome · Pool time

### Day 3 – Amman Children's Museum – Madaba – Dead Sea

Morning at the Children's Museum Jordan, then Madaba, where kids can try making a small mosaic. Continue to the Dead Sea for floating.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Children's Museum Jordan · Mosaic-making, Madaba · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 4 – Dead Sea – Petra

Morning at the resort pools, then a scenic drive to Petra with a stop at Kerak Castle to explore its tunnels.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Resort pools · Kerak Castle tunnels · Wadi Musa

### Day 5 – Petra family tour & overnight

Explore Petra with a family-friendly guide for 2.5 hours, with a carriage ride back up the Siq for little legs. Afternoon free.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Horse carriage through the Siq · Petra Kitchen (optional)

### Day 6 – Little Petra – Wadi Rum, camels & camping

Visit Little Petra, then Wadi Rum for a camel ride, sandboarding on the dunes and a 2-hour jeep safari. Dinner cooked in a zarb underground oven and a night in family tents.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Camel riding · Sandboarding · Zarb dinner

### Day 7 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba

Drive to Aqaba for a glass-bottom boat trip over the coral reef and an afternoon at the beach.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Glass-bottom boat · Coral reef · Beach time

### Day 8 – Aqaba free day

A free day by the Red Sea. Optional visits to the Aqaba Marine Park or snorkelling at the Japanese Garden reef.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Free day · Marine Park (optional) · Snorkelling (optional)

### Day 9 – Aqaba – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast and drive north to Queen Alia International Airport (about 4 hours) for your departure flight. Alternatively, fly from Aqaba King Hussein Airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Desert Highway · Farewell · Departure
