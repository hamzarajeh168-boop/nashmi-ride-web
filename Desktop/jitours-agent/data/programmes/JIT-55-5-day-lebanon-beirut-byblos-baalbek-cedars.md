Lebanon from a Beirut base: Phoenician Byblos, the Jeita Grotto, the Roman temples of Baalbek, Bekaa wineries and the ancient Cedars of God.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Beirut

Upon your arrival at Beirut–Rafic Hariri International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Beirut for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Jeita Grotto – Harissa – Byblos

The limestone caves of Jeita Grotto, the cable car to Harissa and the old port of Byblos.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Jeita Grotto · Harissa · Byblos old port

### Day 3 – Baalbek – Bekaa Valley

The Roman temples of Baalbek, then a winery visit in the Bekaa Valley.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Temple of Bacchus, Baalbek · Bekaa Valley · Ksara caves

### Day 4 – Qadisha Valley – Cedars of God

Drive into the mountains to the Qadisha Valley and the Cedars of God, with a stop at the Gibran Museum in Bcharre.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Cedars of God · Qadisha Valley · Gibran Museum

### Day 5 – Beirut city tour – departure

The National Museum, downtown and the Pigeon Rocks, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** National Museum · Pigeon Rocks · Departure
