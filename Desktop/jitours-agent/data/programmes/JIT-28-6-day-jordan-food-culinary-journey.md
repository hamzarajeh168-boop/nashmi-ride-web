Taste Jordan from street falafel to a Bedouin zarb: cooking classes, a market walk, a farm visit and dinner with local families.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman food walk & cooking class

A guided food walk downtown: falafel at a famous hole-in-the-wall, knafeh, spices and coffee in the souk. In the evening, cook maqluba with a local family and eat together.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Downtown food walk · Knafeh · Cooking class

### Day 3 – Amman – olive farm – Madaba

Visit a family olive farm and press in season, then Madaba for the mosaic map and a mansaf lunch. Evening free.

**Overnight:** Madaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Olive farm · Mansaf lunch · Mosaic, Madaba

### Day 4 – Madaba – Petra, Petra Kitchen

Drive the King's Highway to Petra. Short guided visit of the Siq and the Treasury, then the Petra Kitchen cooking class for dinner.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Petra Kitchen · King's Highway

### Day 5 – Petra – Dead Sea

Morning free in Petra, then drive to the Dead Sea for floating and a farewell dinner.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Free morning, Petra · Floating, Dead Sea · Farewell dinner

### Day 6 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast and transfer to Queen Alia airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
