*Jordan Inspiration Tours · sample programme (demo data for agent training)*

| Item | Details |
|---|---|

| Tour type | Small group or private |
| Category | Adventure – cycling |
| Route | Madaba – Kerak – Dana – Petra – Wadi Rum |
| Starts | Queen Alia International Airport, Amman |

Ride the ancient King's Highway from Madaba to Petra, with a support vehicle, then finish with a desert ride in Wadi Rum.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Ride: Madaba – Mt. Nebo loop (40 km)

Bike fitting, then a loop from Madaba to Mount Nebo and down towards the Dead Sea viewpoints.

**Overnight:** Madaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Bike fitting · Mt. Nebo · Dead Sea viewpoints

### Day 3 – Ride: Wadi Mujib canyon crossing – Kerak (60 km)

Descend and climb out of the great Wadi Mujib canyon to Kerak Castle; transfer to Dana.

**Overnight:** Dana (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Mujib canyon · Kerak Castle · Dana village

### Day 4 – Ride: Dana – Shobak – Petra (70 km)

Along the highlands past Shobak Castle and down to Wadi Musa.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Highland plateau · Shobak Castle · Wadi Musa

### Day 5 – Petra guided tour (rest day from the bike)

Explore Petra on foot with a site guide for 2.5 hours.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Royal Tombs · Rest day

### Day 6 – Ride: Wadi Rum desert tracks (45 km)

Transfer to Wadi Rum and ride the hard-packed desert tracks between the mountains; camp night.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Desert tracks · Lawrence Spring · Desert camp

### Day 7 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba or Amman airport

Transfer to Aqaba or back to Amman airport for your flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the ride
