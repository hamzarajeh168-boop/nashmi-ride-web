The classic route with a night by the Red Sea in Aqaba for snorkelling or a glass-bottom boat trip.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Kerak – Petra

Drive the King's Highway: Madaba and its mosaics, Mount Nebo, then the crusader castle of Kerak with its vaulted halls. Continue to Petra for dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mosaic, Madaba · Kerak Castle · King's Highway

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour & overnight

Explore Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours, then enjoy free time to reach the Monastery or the High Place of Sacrifice. Dinner and overnight in Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · High Place of Sacrifice · The Monastery, Petra

### Day 4 – Petra – Wadi Rum, camel ride, jeep tour & camping

Drive to Wadi Rum. Ride camels for one hour, then continue to the camp on a 2-hour jeep safari. Dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Camel riding, Wadi Rum · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Bedouin camp

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba, Red Sea

After breakfast, drive one hour to Aqaba on the Red Sea. Enjoy a 2-hour glass-bottom boat trip with snorkelling over the coral reef, then free time on the beach or in the old town. Overnight in Aqaba.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Glass-bottom boat, Aqaba · Coral reef snorkelling · Aqaba Castle

### Day 6 – Aqaba – Dead Sea

Drive north along the Dead Sea Highway through Wadi Araba to the Dead Sea. Afternoon of floating, mud bath and the resort pools. Dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Araba · Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath, Dead Sea

### Day 7 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
