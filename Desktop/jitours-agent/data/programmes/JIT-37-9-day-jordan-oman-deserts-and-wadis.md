Two great deserts: Wadi Rum in Jordan and the Wahiba Sands in Oman, with Petra, the turquoise pools of Wadi Shab and the fort of Nizwa.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Petra

Amman Citadel and the Madaba mosaics, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · King's Highway

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum

Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then a 2-hour jeep safari and a night in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Amman – fly to Muscat

Drive back to Amman airport for the flight to Muscat (about 3 hrs.). Meet and assist on arrival.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Muscat · Meet & Assist · Muscat corniche

### Day 5 – Muscat city tour

The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, the Royal Opera House, the Muttrah souk and the corniche.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque · Muttrah souk · Royal Opera House

### Day 6 – Wadi Shab – Wahiba Sands

Swim in the turquoise pools of Wadi Shab, then drive into the Wahiba Sands for a dune drive and a camp night.

**Overnight:** Wahiba Sands (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Shab pools · Dune drive · Desert camp

### Day 7 – Wahiba Sands – Nizwa

Drive to Nizwa through the mountain villages and visit the Nizwa Fort.

**Overnight:** Nizwa (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Nizwa Fort · Mountain villages · Falaj irrigation

### Day 8 – Nizwa souk – Muscat

The Friday goat market (on Fridays) or the Nizwa souk, then back to Muscat.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Nizwa souk · Jabrin Castle · Return to Muscat

### Day 9 – Muscat – departure

Transfer to Muscat International Airport for your departure flight.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
