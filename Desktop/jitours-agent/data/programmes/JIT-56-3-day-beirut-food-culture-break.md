Taste Lebanon: a mezze cooking class, a Beirut street-food walk, the cafés of Mar Mikhael and a fish lunch in Byblos.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Beirut

Upon your arrival at Beirut–Rafic Hariri International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Beirut for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Beirut food walk & mezze class

A morning street-food walk (man'oushe, ka'ak, fresh juice), the National Museum, then a Lebanese mezze cooking class and dinner.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Street-food walk · National Museum · Mezze cooking class

### Day 3 – Byblos – departure

The old port and souk of Byblos with a fish lunch by the sea, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Byblos souk · Fish lunch · Departure
