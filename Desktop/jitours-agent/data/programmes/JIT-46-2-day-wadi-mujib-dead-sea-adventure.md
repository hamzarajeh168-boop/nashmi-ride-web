Wade and swim up the Wadi Mujib gorge to its waterfall, then float in the Dead Sea and soak in the Ma'in hot springs the next morning.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Wadi Mujib Siq Trail – Dead Sea

Drive to the Dead Sea (1 hr.) and walk the Siq Trail in Wadi Mujib with a guide (2–3 hrs.), wading and swimming up to the waterfall. Afternoon floating and the overnight at the resort.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Mujib Siq Trail · Mujib waterfall · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 2 – Dead Sea – Ma'in Hot Springs – Amman

Breakfast, then the hot waterfalls of Ma'in for a morning soak before returning to Amman or the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Hot waterfalls, Ma'in · Thermal pools · Return to Amman
