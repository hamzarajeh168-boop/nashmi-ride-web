Five-star hotels, a private guide and luxury vehicle, a candle-lit Petra experience and a bubble tent under the Wadi Rum stars.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – VIP arrival in Amman

VIP fast-track service at Queen Alia airport and a private transfer in a luxury vehicle to your 5-star hotel. Evening dinner at a rooftop restaurant in Jabal Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** VIP fast-track · Rooftop dinner, Jabal Amman · Luxury hotel

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Petra, private evening

Private visits to the Citadel and Madaba, then Petra. In the evening, a private candle-lit experience at the Treasury with Bedouin music (subject to permit).

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Private Citadel tour · Mosaic, Madaba · Private evening at the Treasury

### Day 3 – Petra private guide – Wadi Rum bubble tent

Petra with a private guide at your own pace, then a transfer to Wadi Rum. Sunset with sparkling juice on a dune, then a private dinner and a night in a panoramic bubble tent.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Private guide in Petra · Sunset on the dunes · Bubble tent

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum hot-air balloon (seasonal) – Dead Sea spa

An optional sunrise hot-air balloon flight over Wadi Rum (Oct–Apr), then drive to the Dead Sea for a 60-minute spa treatment at the resort.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Sunrise balloon (seasonal) · 60-min spa treatment · Dead Sea resort

### Day 5 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
