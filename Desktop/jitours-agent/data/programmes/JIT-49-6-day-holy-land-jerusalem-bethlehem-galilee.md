The life of Jesus from Bethlehem to Galilee: the Old City of Jerusalem, the Church of the Nativity, Nazareth and a boat on the Sea of Galilee.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Jerusalem

Upon your arrival at Ben Gurion Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with Israeli entry and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Jerusalem for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Jerusalem Old City

The Via Dolorosa, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre, the Western Wall and Mount Zion with the Room of the Last Supper.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Via Dolorosa · Church of the Holy Sepulchre · Mount Zion

### Day 3 – Mount of Olives – Bethlehem

The Mount of Olives and the Garden of Gethsemane, then Bethlehem for the Church of the Nativity and Shepherds' Field.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Mount of Olives · Church of the Nativity · Shepherds' Field

### Day 4 – Jericho – Jordan Valley – Galilee

Drive through the Jordan Valley via Jericho and Qasr el-Yahud to Tiberias on the Sea of Galilee.

**Overnight:** Galilee (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Jericho · Qasr el-Yahud · Tiberias

### Day 5 – Sea of Galilee – Capernaum – Nazareth

A boat ride on the Sea of Galilee, the Mount of Beatitudes, Capernaum, Tabgha and the Basilica of the Annunciation in Nazareth.

**Overnight:** Galilee (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Boat on the Sea of Galilee · Capernaum · Basilica of the Annunciation

### Day 6 – Galilee – Ben Gurion Airport

Transfer to Ben Gurion Airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
