A compact loop through the three highlights of Jordan with a night in each: Petra, the desert and the lowest point on earth.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Petra

Pick-up from Amman and drive south via the King's Highway viewpoints to Petra. Check in to your hotel and, if you wish, walk to the Petra Visitor Centre in the evening. Dinner and overnight in Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** King's Highway · Wadi Musa town · Petra by night (optional)

### Day 2 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Spend the morning in Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours, covering the Siq, the Treasury, the Theatre and Qasr Al-Bint. After free time, drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari at sunset, followed by dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Sunset in Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 3 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea – Amman airport

After breakfast, drive to the Dead Sea for several hours of floating and a mud bath at a resort day-use, then continue to Amman airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath · Departure
