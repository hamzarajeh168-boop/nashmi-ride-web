Jerusalem's Old City and a day to Masada and the Dead Sea, in a compact long weekend.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Jerusalem

Upon your arrival at Ben Gurion Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with Israeli entry and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Jerusalem for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Jerusalem Old City

A full day in the Old City with a licensed guide: the four quarters, the Western Wall, the Church of the Holy Sepulchre and the Cardo.

**Overnight:** Jerusalem (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Western Wall · Church of the Holy Sepulchre · The Cardo

### Day 3 – Masada – Dead Sea – airport

Cable car up to the Masada fortress, a float in the Dead Sea at Ein Bokek, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Masada cable car · Floating, Dead Sea · Departure
