## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman downtown – Madaba – Petra

Meet the group for a downtown Amman walk and falafel lunch, then Madaba and on to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Downtown Amman · Falafel lunch · Mosaic, Madaba

### Day 3 – Petra & Monastery hike

A guided morning in Petra with the group guide, then the hike to the Monastery.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery, Petra · Group dinner

### Day 4 – Little Petra – Wadi Rum

Little Petra, then Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep tour and a campfire night.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Little Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Campfire night

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea

Drive north to the Dead Sea for floating and a mud bath.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath · Sunset

### Day 6 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Transfer to Queen Alia airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
