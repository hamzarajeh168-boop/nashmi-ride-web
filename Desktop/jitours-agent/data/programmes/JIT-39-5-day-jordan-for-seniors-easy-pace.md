The highlights at a relaxed rhythm: shorter drives, late starts, two nights in Petra and a horse carriage through the Siq.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Petra

A late start, the Madaba mosaics and a comfortable drive to Petra with rest stops.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mosaic, Madaba · Rest stops · Wadi Musa

### Day 3 – Petra by horse carriage

Ride a horse carriage through the Siq to the Treasury, then a gentle 2-hour walk with your guide along the colonnaded street. Afternoon at leisure.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Horse carriage through the Siq · The Treasury, Petra · Afternoon at leisure

### Day 4 – Petra – Dead Sea

Drive to the Dead Sea via the Dead Sea Highway. Afternoon floating with the resort's beach staff.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Dead Sea Highway · Floating, Dead Sea · Resort pools

### Day 5 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast and transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
