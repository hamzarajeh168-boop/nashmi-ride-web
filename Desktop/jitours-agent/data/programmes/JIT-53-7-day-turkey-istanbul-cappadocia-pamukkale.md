Istanbul's mosques and bazaars, the fairy chimneys and cave hotels of Cappadocia and the white travertine terraces of Pamukkale.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Istanbul

Upon your arrival at Istanbul Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Istanbul for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Istanbul old city

Hagia Sophia, the Blue Mosque, the Basilica Cistern and Topkapı Palace with a licensed guide.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Hagia Sophia · Blue Mosque · Topkapı Palace

### Day 3 – Bosphorus cruise – Grand Bazaar

A Bosphorus boat cruise, the Spice Bazaar and the Grand Bazaar.

**Overnight:** Istanbul (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Bosphorus cruise · Spice Bazaar · Grand Bazaar

### Day 4 – Fly Istanbul – Cappadocia

Morning flight to Kayseri, then the Göreme Open-Air Museum and a check-in at your cave hotel.

**Overnight:** Cappadocia (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Göreme Open-Air Museum · Fairy chimneys · Cave hotel

### Day 5 – Cappadocia valleys & underground city

Walk the Rose and Red valleys, visit an underground city and a pottery workshop in Avanos.

**Overnight:** Cappadocia (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Rose Valley · Underground city · Avanos pottery

### Day 6 – Cappadocia – Pamukkale

A scenic drive west to Pamukkale and the travertine terraces and Roman city of Hierapolis.

**Overnight:** Pamukkale (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Travertine terraces · Hierapolis · Cleopatra's Pool (optional)

### Day 7 – Pamukkale – fly Denizli – Istanbul

Fly from Denizli to Istanbul to connect with your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Istanbul · Farewell · Departure
