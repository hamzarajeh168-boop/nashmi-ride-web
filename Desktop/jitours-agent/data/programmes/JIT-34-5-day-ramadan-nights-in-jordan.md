A gentle Ramadan programme: sightseeing in the cool mornings, iftar with local families, taraweeh at Amman's great mosques and suhoor in a Bedouin tent.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman: King Abdullah I Mosque – Cave of the Seven Sleepers – iftar

Morning visits to the King Abdullah I Mosque and the Cave of the Seven Sleepers. Iftar with a local family and taraweeh prayers.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** King Abdullah I Mosque · Cave of the Seven Sleepers · Family iftar

### Day 3 – Jordan Valley shrines of the Companions – Petra

Visit the shrines of Abu Ubaidah and Mu'adh Ibn Jabal, then drive to Petra for iftar at the hotel.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Shrine of Abu Ubaidah · Shrine of Mu'adh Ibn Jabal · Iftar in Petra

### Day 4 – Petra early visit – Dead Sea, Bedouin suhoor

An early-morning visit to Petra before the heat, then the Dead Sea. Iftar at the resort and suhoor in a Bedouin-style tent.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Early Petra visit · Iftar by the Dead Sea · Bedouin suhoor

### Day 5 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Transfer to the airport according to your flight time.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
