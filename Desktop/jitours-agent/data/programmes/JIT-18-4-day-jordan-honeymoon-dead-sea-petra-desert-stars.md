A slow, romantic itinerary: a couple's spa at the Dead Sea, Petra by candlelight and a private bubble tent in Wadi Rum.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival – Dead Sea, couple's spa

Meet and assist at Queen Alia airport and transfer to the Dead Sea. Welcome cake and room decoration, a couple's massage and a sunset dinner on the terrace.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** Room decoration · Couple's massage · Sunset dinner

### Day 2 – Dead Sea – Petra by night

A relaxed morning of floating, then drive to Petra. In the evening walk the candle-lit Siq to the Treasury for the Petra by Night show.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Petra by night · Candle-lit Siq

### Day 3 – Petra private tour – Wadi Rum bubble tent

Petra with a private guide in the cool of the morning, then Wadi Rum. A private jeep to a sunset dune and a candle-lit dinner for two, then a night in a bubble tent.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · Private sunset jeep · Dinner for two

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Amman airport, departure

Sunrise from your tent, breakfast and a transfer to the airport (4 hrs.).

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Desert sunrise · Farewell · Departure
