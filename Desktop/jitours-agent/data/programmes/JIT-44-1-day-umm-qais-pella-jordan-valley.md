Two Decapolis cities in the green north: Umm Qais above the Sea of Galilee and Pella in the Jordan Valley, with lunch overlooking three countries.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Umm Qais – Pella – Amman

Pick-up at 8:00 AM. Explore Umm Qais (Gadara) and its black basalt theatre, then lunch at the Umm Qais Resthouse with views of the Sea of Galilee and the Golan Heights. Continue to Pella in the Jordan Valley and return at about 5:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** Umm Qais, Gadara · View over the Sea of Galilee · Pella
