Two full days inside Petra: the main city, the Monastery, the High Place of Sacrifice, Little Petra and the candle-lit Petra by Night show.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Petra, Petra by Night

Drive the Desert Highway to Petra. In the evening walk the candle-lit Siq for Petra by Night (Mon, Wed, Thu).

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Desert Highway · Petra by Night · Candle-lit Siq

### Day 3 – Petra full day: city, High Place & Monastery

A full day with a site guide for 4 hours: the Siq, the Treasury, the High Place of Sacrifice trail, then the 800 steps to the Monastery.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** High Place of Sacrifice · The Monastery, Petra · Royal Tombs

### Day 4 – Little Petra – Amman airport

Visit Little Petra (Siq Al-Barid) and the Neolithic village of Beidha, then drive to Amman airport for your flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Little Petra · Beidha village · Departure
