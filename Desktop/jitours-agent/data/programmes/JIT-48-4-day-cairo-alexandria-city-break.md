A short Egypt break: the pyramids of Giza and Saqqara, old Cairo and a night on the Mediterranean in Alexandria.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Cairo

Upon your arrival at Cairo International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Egypt visa and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Cairo for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Giza – Saqqara – Memphis

The Pyramids of Giza and the Sphinx, then the Step Pyramid of Saqqara and the open-air museum of Memphis.

**Overnight:** Cairo (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Pyramids of Giza · Step Pyramid, Saqqara · Memphis

### Day 3 – Cairo – Alexandria

Drive to Alexandria (3 hrs.): the Library of Alexandria, the Citadel of Qaitbay and the Corniche, with a seafood dinner.

**Overnight:** Alexandria (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Library of Alexandria · Citadel of Qaitbay · Corniche

### Day 4 – Alexandria – Cairo airport

The Roman catacombs of Kom El-Shoqafa, then drive back to Cairo airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Catacombs of Kom El-Shoqafa · Pompey's Pillar · Departure
