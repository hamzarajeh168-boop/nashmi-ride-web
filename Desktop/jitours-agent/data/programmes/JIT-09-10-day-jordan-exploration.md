The full country: the green north, the eastern desert castles, Dana Biosphere Reserve, Petra, Wadi Rum, Aqaba and the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Ajloun forest – Jerash – Umm Qais – Amman

A morning walk in the Ajloun Forest Reserve, a visit to Ajloun Castle, then Jerash with a site guide for 1.5 hours and Umm Qais at sunset. Return to Amman.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Ajloun Forest Reserve · Jerash, Oval Plaza · Umm Qais

### Day 3 – Desert Castles – Azraq Wetland – Madaba

Head east to the Umayyad desert castles: Qasr Al-Kharana and the frescoed bathhouse of Qusayr Amra (UNESCO), then the Azraq Wetland Reserve and Qasr Al-Azraq, where Lawrence of Arabia stayed. Drive to Madaba for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Madaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Qusayr Amra frescoes · Azraq Wetland · Qasr Al-Azraq

### Day 4 – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Bethany – Kerak – Dana

Madaba mosaics, Mount Nebo and the Baptism Site, then Kerak Castle. Continue to the Dana Biosphere Reserve for a sunset over the valley and the overnight.

**Overnight:** Dana (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mt. Nebo · Baptism Site · Dana village at sunset

### Day 5 – Dana – Shobak – Petra

Morning hike on the White Dome trail (2–3 hrs., easy) above the Dana valley, then Shobak Castle and on to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** White Dome trail, Dana · Shobak Castle · Wadi Musa

### Day 6 – Petra guided tour & overnight

Explore Petra with a private English-speaking guide for 2.5 hours, then free time for the Monastery. Dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery, Petra · Qasr Al-Bint

### Day 7 – Little Petra – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Visit Little Petra and the Neolithic village of Beidha, then drive to Wadi Rum for a 3-hour jeep safari and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Little Petra · Beidha Neolithic village · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum

### Day 8 – Wadi Rum camel trek – Aqaba

A one-hour camel trek at sunrise, then drive to Aqaba. Afternoon free for the beach, snorkelling or the old town. Overnight in Aqaba.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Camel trek, Wadi Rum · Red Sea beach · Aqaba old town

### Day 9 – Aqaba – Wadi Mujib – Dead Sea

Drive north through Wadi Araba. In season (April–October), wade the Siq Trail in Wadi Mujib (2 hrs.); otherwise visit the Lot's Cave museum. Continue to the Dead Sea for floating and the overnight.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Mujib Siq Trail · Museum at the Lowest Place on Earth · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 10 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
