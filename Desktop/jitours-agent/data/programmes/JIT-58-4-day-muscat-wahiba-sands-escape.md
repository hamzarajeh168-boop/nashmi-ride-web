A short Oman escape: Muscat's mosque and souk, the palm-lined pools of Wadi Bani Khalid and a night in a desert camp.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Muscat

Upon your arrival at Muscat International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Muscat for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Muscat city tour

The Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque, the Muttrah souk and a sunset dhow cruise along the coast.

**Overnight:** Muscat (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Sultan Qaboos Grand Mosque · Muttrah souk · Sunset dhow cruise

### Day 3 – Wadi Bani Khalid – Wahiba Sands

Swim in the pools of Wadi Bani Khalid, then a dune drive and a night in the Wahiba Sands.

**Overnight:** Wahiba Sands (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Bani Khalid · Dune drive · Desert camp

### Day 4 – Wahiba Sands – Muscat airport

Sunrise in the desert and drive back to Muscat airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Desert sunrise · Farewell · Departure
