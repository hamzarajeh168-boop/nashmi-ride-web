A taste of the desert from Aqaba: a 3-hour jeep safari, a one-hour camel ride and a Bedouin lunch.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Aqaba – Wadi Rum – Aqaba

Pick-up at 9:00 AM and drive one hour to Wadi Rum. A 3-hour jeep safari to the Lawrence Spring, Khazali Canyon and the red dunes, a one-hour camel ride and lunch at a Bedouin camp. Back in Aqaba at about 5:00 PM.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Camel ride · Bedouin lunch
