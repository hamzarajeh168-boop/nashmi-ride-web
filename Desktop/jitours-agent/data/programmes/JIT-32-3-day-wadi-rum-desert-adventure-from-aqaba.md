Two full days in the desert: jeep and camel, a scramble to the Um Fruth rock bridge, sandboarding and two nights at a Bedouin camp.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Aqaba – Wadi Rum, sunset jeep tour

Pick-up in Aqaba and drive one hour to Wadi Rum. Afternoon 3-hour jeep tour ending with sunset on the dunes. Dinner and overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Sunset dunes · Bedouin camp

### Day 2 – Camel trek – Um Fruth bridge – sandboarding

A 2-hour camel trek in the morning, a guided scramble on the Um Fruth rock bridge and sandboarding in the afternoon.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Camel trek · Um Fruth rock bridge · Sandboarding

### Day 3 – Wadi Rum sunrise – Aqaba

Sunrise over the desert and breakfast, then transfer back to Aqaba.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Desert sunrise · Bedouin tea · Return to Aqaba
