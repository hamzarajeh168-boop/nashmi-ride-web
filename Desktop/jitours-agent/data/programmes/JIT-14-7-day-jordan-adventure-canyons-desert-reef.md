Canyoning in Wadi Mujib, hiking in Dana, a back-door entry to Petra, scrambling in Wadi Rum and diving in the Red Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival – Dead Sea

Meet and assist at Queen Alia airport and transfer directly to the Dead Sea (45 min.) for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Dead Sea sunset

### Day 2 – Wadi Mujib Siq Trail – Dana

Wade and swim upstream through the red sandstone gorge of Wadi Mujib to the waterfall (2–3 hrs.). Drive to Dana for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Dana (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Mujib Siq Trail · Mujib waterfall · Dana village

### Day 3 – Dana – Wadi Ghuweir canyon hike – Petra

A guided half-day canyon hike in Wadi Ghuweir with water pools and palms, then transfer to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Ghuweir · Canyon pools · Wadi Musa

### Day 4 – Petra via the back trail

Enter Petra from Little Petra on foot to reach the Monastery first, then descend through the city with your guide and exit through the Siq.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Monastery, Petra · The Treasury, Petra · High Place of Sacrifice

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum: Burdah rock bridge scramble & camping

Jeep to Jebel Burdah and scramble up to the 35 m-high natural rock bridge with a Bedouin guide (4 hrs.). Night in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch, dinner

**Highlights:** Burdah rock bridge · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 6 – Wadi Rum – Aqaba, two dives

Drive to Aqaba for two boat dives (or a beginner try-dive) on the Cedar Pride wreck and the Japanese Garden reef.

**Overnight:** Aqaba (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Cedar Pride wreck · Japanese Garden reef · Red Sea

### Day 7 – Aqaba – departure

Transfer to Aqaba King Hussein Airport or drive back to Amman airport (4 hrs.) for your flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the adventure
