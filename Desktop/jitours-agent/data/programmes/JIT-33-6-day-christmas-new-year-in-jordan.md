Spend the holidays in the Holy Land: Christmas Eve in Madaba, the Baptism Site, Petra, a desert night and a New Year's dinner at the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Madaba Christmas Eve

Visit Madaba's mosaic map and the decorated old town, and attend the Christmas Eve celebrations at St. George Church.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mosaic map, Madaba · Christmas lights · St. George Church

### Day 3 – Bethany – Mt. Nebo – Petra

The Baptism Site of Jesus and Mount Nebo, then drive south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Baptism Site, Bethany · Mt. Nebo · King's Highway

### Day 4 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum

Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari and a festive camp dinner.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Festive camp dinner

### Day 5 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea, gala dinner

Drive to the Dead Sea for floating and the resort's festive gala dinner.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Gala dinner · Resort pools

### Day 6 – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast and transfer to Queen Alia airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
