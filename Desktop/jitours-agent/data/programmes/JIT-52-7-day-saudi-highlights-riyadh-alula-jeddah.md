Modern Riyadh and historic Diriyah, the cliffs of the Edge of the World, the tombs of Hegra and the old coral-stone town of Jeddah, with internal flights included.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Riyadh

Upon your arrival at King Khalid International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in Riyadh for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Riyadh (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Diriyah – Riyadh

The UNESCO mud-brick district of At-Turaif in Diriyah, the Masmak Fortress and the National Museum.

**Overnight:** Riyadh (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** At-Turaif, Diriyah · Masmak Fortress · National Museum

### Day 3 – Edge of the World – fly to AlUla

A morning 4x4 trip to the Edge of the World cliffs, then an evening flight to AlUla.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Edge of the World · 4x4 drive · Flight to AlUla

### Day 4 – Hegra – Elephant Rock

Hegra with a licensed guide and sunset at Elephant Rock.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Qasr Al-Farid, Hegra · Nabataean tombs · Elephant Rock

### Day 5 – Fly AlUla – Jeddah, Al-Balad

Flight to Jeddah and a walk through the historic coral-stone town of Al-Balad (UNESCO).

**Overnight:** Jeddah (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Al-Balad, Jeddah · Naseef House · Souq Al-Alawi

### Day 6 – Jeddah Corniche – Red Sea

The Jeddah Corniche, the floating mosque and free time by the Red Sea.

**Overnight:** Jeddah (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Jeddah Corniche · Al-Rahma floating mosque · King Fahd's Fountain

### Day 7 – Jeddah – departure

Transfer to King Abdulaziz International Airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
