Cross from Eilat or start in Aqaba and see Petra and Wadi Rum without driving to Amman.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arava border – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Our representative meets you at the Wadi Araba crossing and assists with border formalities. Drive one hour to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari to the Lawrence Spring, the Little Bridge and the dunes, followed by a Bedouin dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** Wadi Araba crossing · 4x4 safari, Wadi Rum · Stargazing

### Day 2 – Wadi Rum – Petra guided tour

After breakfast, drive to Petra. Explore the ancient Nabataean city with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours, then enjoy free time to climb to the Monastery or the High Place of Sacrifice. Dinner and overnight in Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery, Petra · Royal Tombs

### Day 3 – Petra – Little Petra – Aqaba or Arava border

Breakfast, then a short visit to Little Petra (Siq Al-Barid), the caravan station of the Nabataeans. Continue south to Aqaba or the Wadi Araba border for your onward journey.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Little Petra · Desert Highway views · Departure
