The best-selling Jordan loop with a full day in Petra, a desert night in Wadi Rum and a relaxing resort night at the Dead Sea.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman Citadel – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

Visit the Amman Citadel and the Roman Theatre, then Madaba and its mosaic map, and Mount Nebo with views over the Jordan Valley. Continue south to Petra for dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · Mt. Nebo

### Day 3 – Petra full day – Wadi Rum, camping

A full day in Petra, starting with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours through the Siq to the Treasury and the lower city, then free time to climb to the Monastery. In the late afternoon drive to Wadi Rum for dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · The Monastery, Petra · Bedouin camp

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum jeep safari – Dead Sea

After breakfast, a 3-hour 4x4 safari through Wadi Rum: Lawrence Spring, Khazali Canyon, the sand dunes and the Burdah rock bridge viewpoint. Then drive to the Dead Sea for floating and an overnight at the resort.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Khazali Canyon · Floating, Dead Sea

### Day 5 – Dead Sea – Amman airport, departure

Breakfast at the hotel. According to your flight time, our driver will transfer you from the Dead Sea to Queen Alia International Airport for your departure flight, with farewell.

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
