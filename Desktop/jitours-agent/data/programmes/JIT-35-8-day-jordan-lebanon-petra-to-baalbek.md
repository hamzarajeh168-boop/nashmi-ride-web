Petra and the desert in Jordan, then a short flight to Beirut for the Roman temples of Baalbek, ancient Byblos and the Jeita Grotto.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Petra

Amman Citadel and the Madaba mosaics, then south to Petra.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Amman, The Citadel · Mosaic, Madaba · King's Highway

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum

Petra with a site guide for 2.5 hours, then a 2-hour jeep safari and a night in the Wadi Rum camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 Safari, Wadi Rum · Desert camp

### Day 4 – Fly Amman – Beirut

Drive to Amman airport for the one-hour flight to Beirut. Meet and assist, then an evening walk on the Corniche.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Flight to Beirut · Corniche · Downtown Beirut

### Day 5 – Baalbek & Ksara winery

Drive across the Bekaa Valley to the Roman temples of Baalbek, then a visit to the caves of Ksara.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Temple of Bacchus, Baalbek · Bekaa Valley · Ksara caves

### Day 6 – Jeita Grotto – Harissa – Byblos

The limestone caves of Jeita Grotto, the cable car to Harissa and the ancient port of Byblos.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, lunch

**Highlights:** Jeita Grotto · Harissa · Byblos old port

### Day 7 – Beirut city tour

The National Museum, the downtown mosques and churches, Gemmayzeh and the Pigeon Rocks at sunset.

**Overnight:** Beirut (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** National Museum · Gemmayzeh · Pigeon Rocks

### Day 8 – Beirut – departure

Transfer to Beirut airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Farewell · Departure · End of the tour
