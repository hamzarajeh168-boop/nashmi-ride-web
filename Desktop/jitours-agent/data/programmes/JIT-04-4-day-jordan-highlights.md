Amman, the mosaic city of Madaba, Mount Nebo, Petra, Wadi Rum and the Dead Sea in four well-paced days.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in Amman

Upon your arrival at Queen Alia International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your Jordan visa and handle all airport formalities. You will then meet your driver, who will transfer you to your hotel in Amman for the overnight.

**Overnight:** Amman (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Visa formalities

### Day 2 – Amman – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Petra

After breakfast, a short panoramic tour of Amman, then drive to Madaba, the City of Mosaics, to see the 6th-century map of the Holy Land in St. George Church. Continue to Mount Nebo, where Moses viewed the Promised Land, then drive south to Petra for dinner and overnight.

**Overnight:** Petra (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Mosaic map, Madaba · Mt. Nebo, Moses Memorial · Amman Citadel (panoramic)

### Day 3 – Petra guided tour – Wadi Rum, jeep tour & camping

Explore Petra with an English-speaking site guide for 2.5 hours and enjoy free time. In the afternoon drive to Wadi Rum for a 2-hour jeep safari, dinner and an overnight in the camp.

**Overnight:** Wadi Rum (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** The Treasury, Petra · 4x4 safari, Wadi Rum · Desert sunset

### Day 4 – Wadi Rum – Dead Sea – Amman airport

Breakfast in the camp, then drive to the Dead Sea for floating and swimming at a resort day-use. In the afternoon transfer to Amman airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Floating, Dead Sea · Mud bath · Departure
