Saudi Arabia's open-air museum: the 111 Nabataean tombs of Hegra, the ancient kingdom of Dadan, the old town and sunset at Elephant Rock.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival in AlUla

Upon your arrival at AlUla International Airport, our representative will greet you, assist with your arrival and airport formalities, and transfer you to your hotel in AlUla for the overnight.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options)

**Highlights:** Arrival · Meet & Assist · Hotel check-in

### Day 2 – Hegra (Mada'in Salih)

Explore Hegra, Saudi Arabia's first UNESCO site, including the great tomb of Qasr Al-Farid, then sunset at Elephant Rock.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Qasr Al-Farid, Hegra · Nabataean tombs · Elephant Rock

### Day 3 – Dadan – Jabal Ikmah – AlUla Old Town

The ancient kingdom of Dadan, the inscriptions library of Jabal Ikmah and an evening walk in AlUla Old Town.

**Overnight:** AlUla (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Dadan · Jabal Ikmah inscriptions · AlUla Old Town

### Day 4 – AlUla – departure

Optional morning at the Maraya viewpoint, then transfer to the airport.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Maraya viewpoint (outside only) · Farewell · Departure
