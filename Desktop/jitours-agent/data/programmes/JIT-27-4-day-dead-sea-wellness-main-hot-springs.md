A slow, restorative break: two nights on the Dead Sea shore with spa time, then a night at the waterfalls of the Ma'in hot springs.

## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Arrival – Dead Sea

Meet and assist at Queen Alia airport and transfer 45 minutes to your Dead Sea resort. Afternoon floating and a sunset over the hills of the West Bank.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Dinner

**Highlights:** Arrival · Floating, Dead Sea · Sunset

### Day 2 – Dead Sea spa day

A free day at the resort with one 60-minute treatment per person (massage or mud wrap), pools and a guided mud bath on the beach.

**Overnight:** Dead Sea (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** 60-min spa treatment · Mud bath · Resort pools

### Day 3 – Dead Sea – Ma'in Hot Springs

A short drive up to Ma'in, where hot waterfalls tumble into natural pools. Afternoon in the thermal pools and the spa.

**Overnight:** Ma'in (see hotel options) · **Meals:** Breakfast, dinner

**Highlights:** Hot waterfalls · Thermal pools · Canyon views

### Day 4 – Ma'in – Mt. Nebo – Madaba – Amman airport

Visit Mount Nebo and the mosaics of Madaba on the way to the airport for your departure flight.

**Meals:** Breakfast

**Highlights:** Mt. Nebo · Mosaic, Madaba · Departure
