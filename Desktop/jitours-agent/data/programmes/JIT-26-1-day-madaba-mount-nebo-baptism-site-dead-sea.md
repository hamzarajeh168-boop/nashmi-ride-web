## Itinerary

### Day 1 – Amman – Madaba – Mt. Nebo – Bethany – Dead Sea – Amman

Visit St. George Church and the mosaic map in Madaba, Mount Nebo, then the Baptism Site of Jesus at Bethany Beyond the Jordan. Finish with 2–3 hours at a Dead Sea resort day-use with lunch before returning to Amman.

**Meals:** Lunch

**Highlights:** Mosaic map, Madaba · Baptism Site, Bethany · Floating, Dead Sea
